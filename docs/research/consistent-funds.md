## Mistakes and drawdowns

- **Dynamo:** fell 47.9% in March 2020 and recovered in 150 days ([ETFs Brasil](https://www.etfsbrasil.com.br/fundos/73232530000139)). In 2022, Natura, Alpargatas and Hapvida accounted for about half the loss, and the manager admitted underestimating Natura's problems ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=2072121)). Even so, it beat the Ibovespa in about 85% of 3-year windows over the past two decades.
- **Vokin:** lost about 69% between 2013 and 2015, compounding annual closes, and took until 2019 to recover. Assets fell from R$ 475 mi to R$ 309 mi between Dec/24 and Jun/26.
- **Atmos:** over the last 60 months returned +16.9% vs +57.6% for the Ibovespa. A long bad stretch even for the best fund of the decade.

## What becomes a rule and what is judgment

**Codifiable:** minimum margin of safety (Vokin), net debt/EBITDA cap, minimum return on capital, 15 to 20 stocks with per-position and per-sector caps, cash band, exclusion of state-owned companies.

**Judgment:** assessing the quality of company management (the Natura mistake), deciding when to hedge with NTN-B or dollars, and telling an overdone sell-off from a broken thesis.

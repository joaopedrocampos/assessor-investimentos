# Research methodology and limitations

The conclusions come from 9 funds chosen to cover both successes and failures, not just the top returns. Research done on 26/10/2026.

| Group | Funds | Why they are here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central case | Alaska Black | Success and failure in the same fund: -57% in 2024, +67% in 2025 |
| Consistent | Atmos, Dynamo Cougar, Vokin | Good results across several cycles |
| Recent winners | Infrad Master, Opportunity Ações | Test whether it was skill or timing |
| Failures | Tork, CTM, 3R Cedar II | Long underperformance, illiquidity, poorly controlled risk |

**Sources:** manager letters, fund factsheets and return histories (Mais Retorno, ETFs Brasil, Gorila), CVM data and the financial press. Each research file links its own sources.

## Limitations

- **Portfolio lag.** Funds may withhold positions for up to 90 days in their CVM disclosures. Copying portfolios always arrives late.
- **Survivorship bias.** Funds that followed similar strategies and closed disappear from rankings. That is why the failures group exists.
- **Discretion.** Managers decide by judgment. Only part of their decisions can become rules testable in code.
- **The past does not guarantee the future.** Every rule must pass a backtest across several periods before real money is used.
- **Sources disagree.** In several years, Gorila, ETFs Brasil and the letters show different numbers. The files state which source was used.

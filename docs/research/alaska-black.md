# Alaska Black

Alaska Black wins and loses for the same reason: a single concentrated bet on domestic Brazil, amplified with derivatives and with no declared risk limits. That produced -57.2% in 2024, +67.6% in 2025 and a drop of about 38% between February and August 2026.

Manager: Alaska Investimentos (portfolio manager Henrique Bredda). FIC CNPJ 12.987.743/0001-86; Master 12.055.107/0001-16; Black II 26.648.868/0001-96. Research as of 26/10/2026.

## Portfolio

The letters say the Black portfolio is similar to Alaska Institucional's. In August 2025, the five largest positions added up to about 66% of assets ([Institucional report](https://blobportais.paranabanco.com.br/portalinvestimentos/2026/04/Relatorio-Alaska-Institucional-FIA-Ref.-03.2026.pdf)):

| ASAI3 (Assaí) | 14% | Retail |
| RENT3 (Localiza) | 11% | Car rental |
| POMO4 (Marcopolo) | 10% | Capital goods |

Besides stocks, the fund holds long equity options and is short the US dollar. In 2024 it was also short interest rates, a position closed from 2025 on ([Sep/24 letter](https://www.alaska-asset.com.br/pdf/cartas/Mensais/2024/setembro24.pdf)). The fund rules allow derivatives with no predefined limit relative to assets ([factsheet](https://www.alaska-asset.com.br/pdf/Fundos/BLACK_FIC.pdf)).

In 2021–22 the portfolio was commodities and exporters (Vale, Suzano, Petrobras). In 2024–26 it moved to domestic consumption and rate-sensitive companies.

## What explained each phase

- **2024 (-57.2%):** everything lost together. Retail fell, the options lost value, the Selic rose again against the short-rates position and the dollar rose against the short-dollar position. In December alone, stocks took off 15.4 points and FX 3.1 points ([Dec/24 letter](https://www.alaska-asset.com.br/pdf/cartas/Mensais/2024/dezembro24.pdf)).
- **2025 (+67.6%):** the same bet worked, with the dollar falling and stocks rising. March was +25.4% for the month ([Mar/25 letter](https://www.alaska-asset.com.br/pdf/cartas/Mensais/2025/marco25.pdf)). July (-17.9%) showed the fragility: a 50% US tariff and a rising dollar.
- **2026:** drops in March (war in the Middle East, oil above US$ 100), May (fiscal and electoral uncertainty) and August (retail hit by high rates). September was +22.8% on polls favoring the opposition ([Sep/26 letter](https://www.alaska-asset.com.br/pdf/cartas/Mensais/2026/setembro26.pdf)).
- **26/10/2026:** after Lula's re-election, the Ibovespa fell 13.88% and the dollar rose about 8%. MGLU3 closed at -58%, and ASAI3 and COGN3 fell more than 40% intraday ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/mercados/ibovespa-hoje-bolsa-de-valores-ao-vivo-26102026/)). The impact on the fund's NAV had not been published at research time.

## Stated process

- **Selection:** discounted cash flow for each company; buys when the implied rate of return beats a government bond plus a risk premium ([2023 annual letter](https://www.alaska-asset.com.br/pdf/cartas/2023/2023.pdf)).
- **Concentration:** 20 to 25 stocks, with positions above 15%. The manager admits positions of up to a fifth of the fund ([NeoFeed](https://neofeed.com.br/negocios/para-henrique-bredda-do-alaska-o-proximo-magazine-luiza-e-a-cogna-e-o-proprio-magazine-luiza/)).
- **Risk:** no formal concentration, sector or stop limit has been published. The stated stance is not to sell into weakness.

## Core lesson

The problem was not picking bad stocks: Magazine Luiza made a lot of money in 2016–19 and in 2025. The problem was stacking several bets that depend on the same factor (domestic Brazil, falling rates, falling dollar). When that factor turns, stocks, options and FX all lose at once.
